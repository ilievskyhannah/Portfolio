Did you know?

The bottleneck was one person: the director.

Every researcher who wanted scan time called or emailed him, and he explained it from the start each time. Now the onboarding answers instead of him.

What I'd do differently

I assumed my Figma animations would carry over to Wix.

They didn't, and I rebuilt them in code. Next time I'd test the key buttons and animations in one section before designing the rest.

01The problem

The old site was a page inside the university portal, hard to find and easy to leave. Researchers, me included, gave up on it quickly. To buy or book scan time, they had to call or email the director, who explained it to each of them.

The old site was a page inside the university portal.

  • The homepage: news and links, with no way to book or buy.

  • Booking lived on a separate system page, with no guidance on what to do there.

02What I decided

01 · From the director's inbox

A walkthrough researchers click through

Found
Researchers needed to finish the purchase and booking themselves, without calling anyone.
Chose
A 7-step onboarding that walks them through both. Anyone who used to call the director is sent there instead.

02 · No camera in the scan room

Media built from real sources

Found
A camera can't go into the scan room safely, because metal is pulled into the magnet.
Chose
I built the images and video from real footage, scans and the manufacturer's photos, edited them with AI tools, and said so in the captions.

03 · Who uses it

English by default

Found
The site's users (researchers, biotech companies and international partners) work in English.
Chose
English only. Hebrew was an institutional assumption that the users didn't need.

The full flow, from logging in with university credentials to purchasing scan time.

03What held

The new site stands on its own. About 140 people a month use it, 30% come back, and the booking pages are the most visited after the homepage.

Measured in Microsoft Clarity over the last 30 days, with bot traffic excluded.

Held

The booking pages are the most visited after the homepage: scan purchase (24 visits) and BookitLab (21), against 125 for the homepage. Visitors arrive from TAU sites and israel-cores.org.

Didn't

I can't compare with the old site. It was a page inside the university portal, with no analytics of its own.

Clarity · bots excluded

182 sessions from about 140 people in 30 days, 30% of them returning. Clarity filtered out another 323 bot sessions, which is why I don't use the larger Wix numbers.
