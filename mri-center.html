The problem

The site was a 2013 university template that visitors left within a second. Researchers who wanted to buy or book scan time had to call or email the director, who explained it to each of them.

Solo, end to end

I started the project myself and did the research, information architecture, design system, the build in Wix Studio with custom code, and the media.

Key decisions

0112+ → 0

A walkthrough researchers click through

Found
Researchers needed to finish the purchase and booking themselves, without calling anyone.
Chose
A 7-step onboarding that walks them through both. Anyone who used to call the director is sent there instead.

Media built from real sources

Found
A camera can't go into the scan room safely, because metal is pulled into the magnet.
Chose
I built the images and video from real footage, scans and the manufacturer's photos, edited them with AI tools, and said so in the captions.
0356% from abroad

English by default

Found
The people who use the site, researchers, biotech companies and international partners, work in English.
Chose
English only. Hebrew was an institutional assumption that the users didn't need.
What I got wrong

My Figma animations didn't work in Wix

I designed the animations and buttons as if they'd carry over as they were. I had to rebuild the buttons in Wix and write animation code to make them work.

Next time: I'd build one test section in Wix first, with the key buttons and animations, before designing the rest.
