More about me

I'm a UX/UI Designer & Product Designer with an MFA from the prestigious Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design and an MBA from the College of Management Academic Studies (Colman). Across every project - whether I'm one voice on a team of sixteen or the entire team, research through code - I look for the problem hiding behind the brief: a director buried in repeat emails, a data bug heatmaps couldn't catch, therapists who needed a reflective question instead of an answer.

Outside of product design and UX research, I'm deeply passionate about fine art, painting, sculpture, and music (having sung as a vocalist for many years). I'm also an active sports enthusiast - especially on the tennis court and in the swimming pool.
