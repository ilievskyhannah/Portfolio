The problem

The brief was to expand the platform, but the team couldn't trust its own usage data. Users moved between the platform and social media, so heatmaps and surveys only showed half of each session.

7 interviews

The lead designer felt the two surveys were enough. I pushed for interviews and ran them with the team's UX designer.

Key decisions

01From the interviews

A personal profile for every user

Found
Users wanted content that fit them and wanted to see their own impact. Everyone was anonymous, so the platform couldn't do either.
Chose
Sign-up, sharing preferences and a profile with impact, reporting and settings. It added a step before the first post, and we accepted that.

02For the data problem

Reporting from inside the profile

Found
The team couldn't trust its own usage data.
Chose
Users can report a group or a technical issue from their profile, so the team hears about problems directly.
03From the usage data

A desktop version built for posting

Found
There was no desktop version, and posting was what users did most.
Chose
I designed the posting flow for 7 screen sizes across tablet and desktop.
What I got wrong

I assumed our older users would prefer desktop

Our users are 60 to 90, so desktop seemed the obvious choice. They reached the product through WhatsApp reminders on their phones, and many never knew there was a desktop site.

Next time: I'd put a desktop link in the WhatsApp reminders and show the other ways in during our webinars.
